Where do the iterates go?

Draw a Gaussian dataset once. Each step picks a minibatch and multiplies by \(I-\eta H_t\). Move \(\eta\) and \(B\), then press Run. The cloud is the iterate law; the left table says whether the linear map shrinks or grows.

$$x_{t+1}=(I-\eta H_t)\,x_t-\eta\Big(\tfrac{c_{4,t}}{6}\|x_t\|^2+\tfrac{c_{6,t}}{120}\|x_t\|^4\Big)x_t+\sigma\,\xi_t, \qquad H_t=\frac1B\sum_{i\in\mathcal B_t}z_i z_i^\top, \quad z_i\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma),\quad \Sigma=\mathrm{diag}(\mu_1,\mu_2)$$
\(B=N\): full batch, \(H_t\) is fixed. \(B<N\): \(H_t\) changes every step (matrices usually do not commute). \(c_4,c_6\) can keep the cloud bounded even when the linear map expands. Color bar on \(\eta\): teal = mean-square stable, then Lyapunov edge, then past it.
